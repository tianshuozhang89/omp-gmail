Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

OMP Agentic Gmail ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner, Tianshuo (Tony) Zhang, with his own Google account.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app uses the Gmail API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify to read, search, label, draft and send email in the owner's mailbox.

How data is used and stored

  • OAuth credentials are stored only on the owner's computer, in files readable only by the owner.
  • Email content is processed on the owner's computer at the owner's request. Content needed to answer a request may be sent to the AI model provider the owner has chosen for his assistant.
  • The app runs no server, keeps no database, and collects no analytics.
  • Data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than the owner's own email tasks.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
