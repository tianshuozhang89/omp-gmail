OMP Agentic Gmail

OMP Agentic Gmail is a private, single-user tool. It lets its owner, Tianshuo (Tony) Zhang, read, search, organise and draft email in his own Gmail account from a command-line AI assistant (omp) running on his own Mac.

It is not offered to the public, and no other accounts can sign in to it.
